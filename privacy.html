A LITTLE CLARITY / LAST UPDATED 18 OCTOBER 2026

Your information,
handled with care.

This page explains what happens when you contact Madeform, an independent web design studio in Hyderabad, India.

When you send an enquiry

We collect the name, business name, email address, selected service, and project details you provide. We use these details to understand your project and respond to your enquiry. Submitting a form does not subscribe you to marketing.

When the enquiry form is connected, your submission is sent through Google Apps Script and saved in Madeform’s private Google Sheet with a reference and submission time. We do not sell your information. Access is restricted to Madeform and authorised Google account collaborators. We keep enquiries while needed for the conversation and any resulting work, and review records for deletion when they are no longer needed.

Basic protection

The form uses validation, a hidden spam field and a unique request reference to prevent accidental duplicate submissions. The form handler does not store your IP address in the enquiry sheet. Google may process connection information under its own service terms.

WhatsApp and external websites

Choosing a WhatsApp link opens a chat with Madeform at +91 63014 38905. Any message you send there is processed by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms. The Aster Coffee demo opens on GitHub Pages and is a separate website. External services may receive standard connection information when you visit them.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not add marketing trackers or analytics cookies. GitHub Pages and Google may process standard technical information to host the site and handle form submissions.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your enquiry details, or ask a question about their use. Contact Madeform on WhatsApp . Please avoid sending passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information in the project form.

Changes

We may update this page as our website or services change. The date above identifies the latest version.
